Commissions

One-of-a-kind stained and fused glass created collaboratively — designed to live with light, meaning, and intention.

What I Create

I work with clients to create custom glass pieces for homes, gifts, memorials, and special spaces. Commissions range from small suncatchers to large window panels.

  • Stained glass panels and suncatchers
  • Fused glass decorative or functional pieces
  • Memorial, symbolic, or story-driven designs
  • Site-specific pieces for windows or lighted display

The Commission Process

You don’t need a finished idea to get started. Some clients come with sketches or references; others begin with a feeling, memory, or space in mind.

  1. Initial conversation — we talk about your idea, size, placement, and budget range
  2. Concept & sketch — I develop a design direction and refine it with you
  3. Glass selection — color, texture, and transparency choices are confirmed
  4. Creation — I build the piece and share progress updates
  5. Completion — delivery or pickup, plus care and display guidance

What Clients Appreciate

  • Clear communication and thoughtful collaboration
  • Guidance when ideas feel vague or overwhelming
  • Careful attention to color, texture, and light
  • Work that feels personal, intentional, and enduring

Many clients come back for additional pieces after their first commission.

Pricing & Timing

Pricing depends on size, complexity, materials, and timeline. I’m always happy to discuss options and suggest approaches that work within your comfort range.

Typical timelines vary depending on scope and current workload, but I’ll always give you a clear estimate before work begins.
