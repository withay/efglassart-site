New Hampshire Renaissance Faire (May)

Each spring I create a focused run of smaller pieces for the New Hampshire Renaissance Faire. These are designed to be easy to display, gift, and bring home — and they tend to go fast during the event.

  • Small stained-glass suncatchers and geometric designs
  • Fantasy-inspired themes (when available)
  • Limited-run quantities (I may revisit favorites year to year)

If you’re attending the Faire and want pickup, message me early so I can set a piece aside.

Limited Drops / Seasonal Runs
