New Hampshire Renaissance Faire (May)

Each spring I create a focused run of smaller pieces for the New Hampshire Renaissance Faire. They’re designed to be easy to display, gift, and bring home — and they tend to go fast during the event.

Danville Holiday Craft Fair + Local Delivery

After Thanksgiving I do the local Danville, NH holiday craft fair. After the event, I’ll often post a small “leftovers” batch online — ornaments and ready-to-go pieces — and can deliver locally.

Sunapee Rock Shop (Year-Round Wholesale)

Sunapee Rock Shop in Sunapee, NH carries my work year-round. I create several designs exclusively for them, including “flowers on a stick,” feathers (large and small), squares, infinity hearts, and more. (Availability varies in-store.)
